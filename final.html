One last thing
before you go.

The photos are lovely. The videos are excellent. But this is the bit I really wanted you to have.

For you, on your birthday

My Niel,

Sometimes I wish I could take you through my memories and let you see yourself the way I see you in them. Not just in the pictures, but in the moments around them. The walks home, the gardens, the meals, the laughter over things that probably wouldn't make sense to anybody else. The times life wasn't particularly kind to either of us, and somehow, we still found reasons to laugh, or bowed our heads and prayed.

I don't know when these little things became so precious to me. I only know that somewhere along the way, they took root. And now, when I think of the life I've lived with you in it, I find so many ordinary moments I wouldn't trade for anything grand.

You've been there for me when my mind has been crowded with worry, when things have felt heavy, when I haven't had much encouragement left for myself, you've reminded me that I don't have to carry everything on my own. Thank you for always holding my hands to pray with me.

I hope you know that I see you, too. Not only the parts of you that are easy to celebrate, but the man who is still figuring things out, carrying his own thoughts, making plans, and trying to build a life. I hope you give yourself some of the grace you so readily need from life. Even trees have seasons when all they seem to do is stand there, yet something is happening beneath the ground.

I wish I could do more for you today. You know, I've had so many ideas in my head about how I wanted to celebrate you, and it hurts a little that I can't bring all of them to life right now. But I kept thinking about what I could do. And somehow, that thought brought me here, gathering the people who know you, collecting their words, keeping our memories, and making you this little corner of the world where you can see just how much of a difference your life makes.

Maybe it isn't everything I imagined. But it is something I made with you in mind, and I hope you feel that.
